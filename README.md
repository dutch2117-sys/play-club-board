# Play Club Sign-In Board

A drag-and-drop morning check-in board for elementary classrooms. Project it on a smartboard, and each student drags their name tag to the play club they're choosing for the day, then taps hot or cold lunch.

## Features

- Drag-and-drop name tags that work with touch, pen, or mouse, and with several students at once on multi-touch boards
- Every name stays visible without scrolling; tags resize to fit the screen
- Optional lunch choice (hot/cold) with live counts at the top of the board
- A "Today's lunch" list in settings for your lunch count
- Automatically clears each morning (can be turned off)

## Customize it for your classroom

Click the ⚙️ button in the top corner of the board:

- **Play clubs:** rename them, change colors and icons, reorder them, add new ones (up to 8), or remove ones you don't use
- **Lunch choice:** turn it on or off, and rename or recolor the two options (for example, "School lunch" and "Home lunch")
- **Board title:** change "Play Club" to anything you like, such as "Morning Choice"
- **Class list:** paste your students' names, one per line

Tip: to type an emoji icon, press **Windows key + .** (period) on Windows or **Control + Command + Space** on a Mac.

## Privacy

Student names and settings are saved only in the browser on the computer you use. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing is shared with other teachers who use the same link. Each teacher's board is private to their own computer and browser.

Because settings live in the browser, clearing your browser data or switching to a different computer or browser will start you over with the default setup.

## Using it

Open the link, click ⚙️, add your class list, and you're ready. Press **F11** (or your browser's full-screen option) when projecting.
